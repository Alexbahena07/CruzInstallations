Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical flooring installation take?

For a typical room around 200 sq ft, installation usually takes about 2–3 hours. It can run a bit longer if furniture needs to be moved out of the space first. For larger jobs or multiple rooms, we'll give you a clear time estimate during your free quote.

Do you remove and haul away my old flooring?

Yes — removing and hauling away your old flooring is included as part of the job, so you don't have to worry about disposal.

Do I need to move my furniture before you arrive?

Not necessarily — we're happy to move furniture for you as part of the job. Just let us know ahead of time so we can plan for it.

What's the best flooring for Chicago basements or homes with kids/pets?

For most basements and homes with kids or pets, we recommend SPC or LVP (luxury vinyl plank). Both are 100% waterproof, which makes them a great choice for protecting against pet accidents, spills, and moisture. That said, the right fit depends on your space — we'll walk you through your options when we take a look.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — Cruz Installations & Remodeling LLC is fully licensed and insured, so you can have peace of mind throughout your project.

Do you offer a warranty on your installation?

Yes — we back our installation work with a 1-year warranty for your peace of mind.
